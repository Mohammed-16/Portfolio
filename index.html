About me

Benefit rules change, and an AI that answers from last year's law is worse than no AI. I build LLM assistants that answer from the official legal text and keep themselves up to date when the law changes, with a human reviewing every update.

At LeistungsLotse in Berlin I build an AI assistant for Wohngeld and BAföG questions and own the legal content pipeline end to end: a nightly agent that detects law amendments and opens a pull request with the rewritten paragraphs, an evaluation setup that compares 15+ LLMs (including Gemini, Qwen, GLM and DeepSeek) on real Wohngeld cases, and OCR ingestion that feeds retrieval.

Before that I spent about 1.5 years shipping Ethereum smart contracts and React apps in India, so I'm used to code where a mistake costs real money. I'm finishing my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg and I'm looking for a full-time AI Engineer role or a company master's thesis in Germany.
